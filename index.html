        🧮 How the pick works

        • The pool: 45 common dinner ideas plus your own list, narrowed by your filters. The number of matches is shown above, and you can open the list to see every one.
        • Fair: the pick uses your browser’s cryptographic random number generator with rejection sampling, so every idea in the pool has exactly the same chance. The spin animation only reveals the result.
        • No repeats: your last five dinners marked Sounds good are skipped, the idea you just saw is skipped when you spin again, and hidden ideas are never picked. If that would leave nothing, recent dinners are allowed again.
        • Quick and vegetarian: quick applies to home cooking only and means dishes that usually take about 30 minutes or less. Vegetarian means no meat or fish; for ordering in, it means places where vegetarian choices are usually on the menu.

        ❓ Frequently asked questions

        Is the pick really random?

        Yes. Every idea that matches your filters has the same chance. The pick uses your browser’s cryptographic random number generator with a method that avoids bias, and the spinning animation only shows the result; it does not change it.

        Can I add my own restaurants or dishes?

        Yes. Add them under My list and choose whether each one is cooked at home or ordered in. Select only the My list filter to pick from your own options, or leave all filters off to mix them with the built-in ideas.

        How does it avoid repeats?

        When you press Sounds good, the meal is saved to your recent dinners, and your last five dinners are skipped. Spin again never lands on the same idea twice in a row. Never suggest hides an idea until you bring it back. If every match was eaten recently, repeats are allowed so you still get a pick.

        What counts as quick or vegetarian?

        Quick means home-cooked ideas that usually take about 30 minutes or less. Vegetarian means dishes without meat or fish, and for ordering in, kinds of places where vegetarian choices are usually on the menu. Always check ingredients and menus yourself, especially for allergies.

        Is my list saved?

        Yes, in this browser only. Your own ideas, hidden ideas and recent dinners stay on this device and are never sent to us. Use the remove buttons or Clear recent dinners to delete them.

        ⚠️ Please note

        These are general dinner ideas, not nutrition or dietary advice. Labels like vegetarian are general descriptions, so always check ingredients and restaurant menus yourself, especially if you have a food allergy.
